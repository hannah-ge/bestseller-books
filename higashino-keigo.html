Keigo Higashino

东野圭吾

February 4, 1958 — July 23, 2026

Keigo Higashino was one of Japan's most celebrated and prolific mystery authors, born in Osaka and initially trained as an engineer before winning the prestigious Edogawa Rampo Prize for his debut novel After School (1985). He is best known internationally for The Devotion of Suspect X, which earned him the Naoki Prize, and for his beloved "Detective Galileo" and "Detective Kaga" series. Over a career spanning more than 40 years, he published over 100 books and sold more than 100 million copies in Japan alone.
东野圭吾，日本著名推理小说家，出生于大阪，毕业于大阪府立大学工学部，后凭借处女作《放课后》荣获第31届江户川乱步奖，从此走上专职写作的道路。代表作包括《嫌疑人X的献身》《白夜行》《解忧杂货店》等，其中《嫌疑人X的献身》荣获第134届直木奖。他的作品以精密的逻辑构架和深刻的人性洞察著称，多部小说被改编为电影与电视剧，深受全球读者喜爱。
